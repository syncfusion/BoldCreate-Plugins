# Fill Series

> Automatically fill cells with data that follows a pattern in WPF Spreadsheet control.

---

## Overview

Supports filling a range of cells with a sequence or pattern, such as numbers, dates, or custom lists.

---

## Key Features
- Auto-fill numbers, dates, days, months
- Custom fill patterns
- Drag fill handle to extend series

---

## Example

Enter '1' in a cell, drag the fill handle down to auto-fill with '2', '3', etc.
